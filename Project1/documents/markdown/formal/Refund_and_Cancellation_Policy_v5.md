# Refund and Cancellation Policy

Governing pilot merchant policy for order cancellations, customer return windows, and partial refund calculations.

## Order Cancellation Guidelines

Customers may initiate self-service cancellations while an order remains in PENDING or AUTHORIZED state. Once warehouse packing commences, cancellation is disabled and the customer must utilize the return workflow.

## Meridian Return Window

For Meridian Outfitters, customer return requests may be opened within 30 calendar days of documented carrier delivery. Returned goods are subject to merchant warehouse inspection.

## Partial Refund Computation

Partial line-item refunds cannot exceed the total captured transaction amount minus previous settled refunds. A refund is considered completed only upon PulsePay webhook settlement confirmation.

## Policy Lineage

This policy supersedes the outdated 14-day return window stated in superseded ADR-001.
